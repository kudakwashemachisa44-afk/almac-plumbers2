About ALMAC

From ALMAC Plumbers to ALMAC Contractors

Plumbing is the core service we have been offering for more than 22 years. Since 2004, ALMAC has built an established reputation for quality, service and customer satisfaction across Zimbabwe.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free. For small jobs we can often quote from photos sent on WhatsApp; for bigger jobs we visit the site first so the quote is accurate.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Harare and work across Harare, Chitungwiza, Ruwa, Norton and surrounding areas. Larger projects are taken on throughout Zimbabwe.

Do you work on homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We work on houses, flats, offices, shops, schools and new developments, from small repairs to complete plumbing installations.

Can you supply the materials?

Yes. We can include quality materials and fittings in your quote, or install fittings you have already bought. We'll advise you on what works best.

Is ALMAC Contractors the same company as ALMAC Plumbers?

Yes. ALMAC Plumbers is now ALMAC Contractors. Same team, same phone number and the same 22+ years of plumbing experience, with a wider range of contracting work.

What is the fastest way to reach you?

Call or WhatsApp Brian on 077 223 4089. Sending a few photos of the problem on WhatsApp helps us respond faster.
